# Code of conduct

We want people to be able to ask questions, contribute, and disagree respectfully.
This applies to this project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project-managed spaces, and to people representing the project publicly.

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and personal attacks
  are not acceptable.

To report a concern privately, email [support@voiceflow.com](mailto:support@voiceflow.com)
with this repository's URL and ask for the project maintainer. Include only the
information needed to understand the concern. Do not post sensitive reports in
public issues. For security vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers review reports in context, limit sharing to what is needed to address
them, and may edit or remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation.
Consequences should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior.
Maintainers are subject to these expectations too; if a concern involves a
maintainer, identify that conflict in the private report so another reviewer
can handle it. Reports are handled on a best-effort basis, without a guaranteed
response time.
